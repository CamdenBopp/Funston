The books download once into the fixture cache outside the repository; none is committed. Each book: it opens, its contents are listed from the source the study predicts, every readable spine item renders with no lost text (every source character outside hidden content appears in the rendered chapter, compared case-insensitively because small-caps styling uppercases), the first pages of the first chapter files carry the expected entry, a saved position in the middle of the book restores exactly after a text size change by the offset path and by the CFI path, and the expected diagnostics are present. The run also answers Spec open question 5: whether TextKit's suspicious-contents guard moves any page break.
